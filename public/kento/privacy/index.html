Privacy Policy — Kento

Last updated: 30 September 2026 App: Kento (com.borakececi.offlinebadukai) Data controller: Lysa Software Media — Bora Kececi Contact: rbkececi@gmail.com

In short

This app works offline. Your games, your analyses and your photos stay on your device. The AI analysis runs on your own phone's processor; no game is ever sent to a server.

The one exception is the Cloud feature, which only you can start: if you sign in and upload a game, that game is stored in our Firebase project. It is entirely optional and the rest of the app works without it.

1.3 Error reports

If something goes wrong inside the app, we can be told about it — you decide. The switch is in Settings → Privacy → "Send error reports". It is on by default and turning it off takes effect immediately.

datawhystored in
The error and where in the code it happenedTo fix the faultFirebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)
Device model, OS version, app versionThe same fault often affects only one device or one versionFirebase Crashlytics
An install identifier generated by CrashlyticsTo count how many people hit the same faultFirebase Crashlytics

What is never sent: your games, your analyses, your photos, your name or your email. Where an error message would contain a file name — and in this app a file name is usually the title you gave a game — the name is removed before the report leaves the device.

The identifier is generated by Crashlytics for this install only. It is not an advertising ID, it is not linked to your account, and reinstalling the app produces a new one.

1.4 Purchases

In-app purchases go through the App Store and Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the store only tells us whether a purchase is valid.

1.5 Photos you send to improve recognition

If you marked the board corners by hand while reading a position from a photo, the result screen offers "Help improve recognition". It works only when you tap "Send"; no photo is ever sent on its own. Before the first send you are told what is sent and asked to agree; you can withdraw that at any time with Settings → Privacy → "Share recognition photos".

datawhywhere
The photo itself (downscaled, EXIF removed)To train the board recognition modelCloud Firestore (Google LLC), until reviewed
The corners you marked and the board sizeThe correct answer for the modelCloud Firestore
The app's own corner guessTo see where the model went wrongCloud Firestore
Operating system and app versionTo see which devices have troubleCloud Firestore

Not sent: your account, name, e-mail, location, and the location and device data inside the photo (EXIF is stripped completely before sending). The submission is anonymous; the photo is not tied to any account or installation ID.

Photos showing a person are not sent. Face detection runs on the device before sending; if even one face is found, the photo does not leave your device and you are told so. Photos with no board are not sent either.

Photos are used only to improve board recognition and are not shared with third parties. The developer reviews every photo by hand; once reviewed, it is deleted from the cloud and kept only in the developer's own training set. A photo showing a person that slips through is deleted without entering the training set.

2. What we do not collect

  • No advertising ID, ad network or tracking pixel
  • No analytics of how you use the app: no screen views, no session lengths, no feature counters. The only thing that can be reported is a fault, and only if you leave the switch on (section 1.3)
  • No location, contacts or calendar access. The microphone is used only while voice move input is on and you have started listening; audio is never sent anywhere
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties

4. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Photos you send to improve recognition (1.5) stay in the cloud until the developer has reviewed them, then they are deleted from the cloud.

Your cloud games stay until you delete them. You can delete your account and every cloud game from inside the app: Library → Cloud → "Delete my account". Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

5. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, objection to the processing of, and portability of your data.

For cloud data, deletion and access are immediate from inside the app. For anything else write to rbkececi@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days at the latest.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — for the cloud feature and for recognition photos you choose to send (see 1.5). Data is held in the European region. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — error reports only, and only while the switch in Settings → Privacy is on. See section 1.3. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchase validation.
